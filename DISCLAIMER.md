# Disclaimer & Responsible Use

DecompFlow is an open-source research and engineering tool built strictly for:
- Authorized security auditing and vulnerability research.
- Interoperability analysis and protocol documentation.
- Legacy software preservation, decompilation, and recovery of lost source code.
- Educational study of compilers, binary structures, and runtime architectures.

## Responsible Conduct

Users of DecompFlow must ensure that their activities comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws, regulations, and third-party software licensing agreements.

- **Explicit Authorization:** Only analyze software, binaries, or web services that you own or for which you have explicit, documented permission to inspect.
- **No Malicious Use:** DecompFlow must not be used to bypass license enforcement systems, defeat copyright management protections, generate exploit payloads, or distribute malicious software.
- **Third-Party Engines:** Third-party decompilation and inspection tools (such as Ghidra, REA, and Playwright) are governed by their respective licenses and terms of service.
